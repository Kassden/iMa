Prediction and wagering pipeline

The horse model and market remain independently measurable, then combine before finishing-order and pool probabilities are calculated.

Point-in-time inputsHorse form, rating, draw, weight, distance, class, going, trackwork and veterinary history
->
Feature transformationRace-safe joins, missing-value indicators, scaling and categorical encoding
->
Fundamental probabilityLogistic or boosted model, normalized within each race and temperature calibrated
Live WIN market oddsOnly odds observed at or before the prediction timestamp
->
Market probabilityInverse odds, takeout-aware normalization and race-level probability scaling
Fitted market blendFundamentalwf x Marketwm, with weights learned on validation races
Public fallbackFirst-time or unratable horses inherit their public probability
->
Finishing-order modelFitted Benter second- and third-place exponents
->
Pool probabilitiesRank every valid single-horse or combination outcome
WIN / PLACE / QIN / QPL / TRI / TIERCE / FIRST4 / QUARTET

->
